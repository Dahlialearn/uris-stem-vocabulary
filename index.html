PolyU Undergraduate Research and Innovation Scheme

Computer-Assisted STEM Vocabulary Learning: Selection, Morphology, and Spacing

A PolyU URIS project investigating computer-assisted medical English vocabulary learning for Chinese-speaking learners through research-informed word selection, morphological support, and spacing schedules.

Explore the vocabularyRead about the research

Why vocabulary matters

Technical language shapes access to STEM.

STEM stands for Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics.

Disciplinary vocabulary helps students read technical texts, develop subject knowledge, and communicate precisely within these fields.

Medical and technical vocabulary can be challenging when learners are unfamiliar with English word structure. This project examines whether morphological support and spacing schedules can provide a more structured approach to digital vocabulary learning.

Project focus

Vocabulary selection

Words are adapted from discipline-specific STEM vocabulary and corpus-based academic vocabulary resources.

Morphological analysis

Target words are classified as simple, derived, or compound, with prefixes and suffixes identified where applicable.

Spacing schedules

Distributed and massed presentation schedules are compared.

Digital flashcards

Each target word is presented three times through a structured digital flashcard activity.
