Computer-Assisted STEM Vocabulary Learning: Selection, Morphology, and Spacing

Developing and evaluating medical and life-science vocabulary through corpus-informed selection and digital flashcards.

Explore the vocabularyRead about the research

Why vocabulary matters

Technical language shapes access to STEM.

STEM stands for Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics.

Students need disciplinary vocabulary to read technical texts, understand concepts, and communicate within these fields. This project examines carefully selected words through morphology, spaced learning, and digital practice.

Project focus

Word selection

Candidate words are adapted from discipline-focused and corpus-based vocabulary resources.

Morphology

Word structure supports analysis of prefixes, suffixes, and morphological categories.

Spacing

The study compares distributed and massed exposure schedules.

Digital learning

Target words are studied through structured digital flashcards.
