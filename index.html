Why vocabulary matters

Technical language shapes access to STEM.

STEM stands for Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics, four connected areas of education and research.

Students need disciplinary vocabulary to read technical texts, understand concepts, and communicate within these fields. This project examines how carefully selected words can be taught through structured digital practice.

Project focus

STEM-specific

Words are drawn from discipline-focused and academic corpus resources.

Morphology-based

Recorded prefixes, suffixes, and word structure support focused exploration.

Research-informed

Corpus evidence, expert ratings, and vocabulary-learning research guide the work.
