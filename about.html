Research

Research focus

This project investigates computer-assisted medical English vocabulary learning among Chinese-speaking learners. It combines research-informed word selection, morphological elaboration, and distributed or massed presentation schedules in a digital flashcard task.

Vocabulary selection

The selected words are adapted from the HKUST STEM Vocabulary resource and cross-referenced with academic vocabulary records from the Corpus of Contemporary American English (COCA). This process produced a 138-word candidate pool for expert evaluation. Word-level ratings are currently available for 137 words, and a 34-word subset was implemented in the learning study. Frequency values reported for the 34-word study set were obtained from the iWeb Corpus.

Study design

  • The 34 target words are classified as simple, derived, or compound.
  • Morphological elaboration makes relevant word structures, prefixes, and suffixes explicit to learners.
  • The 2 × 2 between-participants design crosses spacing schedule (distributed or massed) with morphological elaboration (with or without elaboration).
  • Participants encounter each target word three times.

Expert ratings

Fifty-eight expert raters affiliated with PolyU’s STEM faculties, including professors, instructors, and PhD candidates, rated the candidate words for instructional usefulness and perceived difficulty on 7-point scales.

Research status

Study in progress.
