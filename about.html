About the project

Research

A PolyU URIS project on STEM vocabulary selection, morphology, spacing, and digital learning.

Research motivation

  • Support access to disciplinary reading and communication.
  • Examine a focused set of medical and life-science-related vocabulary.
  • Investigate morphology and learning schedules through digital flashcards.

Vocabulary sources

The initial vocabulary pool was adapted from the HKUST STEM Vocabulary resource and refined through cross-referencing with academic vocabulary data from the Corpus of Contemporary American English (COCA).

  • HKUST provided discipline-specific STEM vocabulary.
  • COCA supported academic-domain cross-referencing and source-recorded part-of-speech information.
  • A formal 138-word candidate pool proceeded to expert ratings.
  • The implemented digital-flashcard study uses 34 target words.

Morphology, spacing, and learning

  • The 34 study words are classified as simple, derived, or compound.
  • Recorded prefixes and suffixes support focused word-structure exploration.
  • The implemented study crosses distributed or massed spacing with or without morphological elaboration.
  • Each target word receives three digital-flashcard exposures.

Expert ratings

Experts rated instructional usefulness and perceived difficulty on 7-point scales. The website reports verified word-level means only where those data are available.

Research status

The learning study is ongoing. Preliminary analyses are not presented here as finalized findings.
