About the project

Research

A PolyU URIS project on STEM vocabulary, word structure, and digital vocabulary learning.

Research motivation

  • Support access to disciplinary reading and communication.
  • Examine a focused set of medical and life-science-related vocabulary.
  • Investigate structured learning through digital flashcards.

Vocabulary sources

The project used the HKUST STEM Vocabulary resource alongside the Corpus of Contemporary American English (COCA) academic vocabulary data.

  • HKUST provided discipline-specific STEM vocabulary.
  • COCA supported academic-domain cross-referencing and part-of-speech information.
  • The recorded Medicine overlap contains 468 words; the HKUST Life Science list contains 433 words. They share 86 words.
  • A formal 138-word candidate pool proceeded to expert ratings.
  • The current digital-flashcard study uses 34 target words.

Morphology and learning

  • Recorded prefixes and suffixes organize recurring word structures.
  • The implemented study crosses spacing with morphological elaboration.
  • Each target word receives three digital-flashcard exposures.

Expert ratings

Experts rated instructional usefulness and perceived difficulty on 7-point scales. The website reports verified word-level means only where those data are available.

Research status

The learning study is ongoing. Preliminary analyses are not presented here as finalized findings.

Selected references

  • He, L. (2019). Choosing words to teach: A novel method for vocabulary selection and its practical application. TESOL Quarterly.
  • Anglin, J. M. (1993). Vocabulary development: A morphological analysis. Monographs of the Society for Research in Child Development.
