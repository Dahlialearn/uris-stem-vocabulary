Research

Research focus

Disciplinary vocabulary is central to reading, concept development, and communication in STEM. This project examines medical and life-science vocabulary through corpus-informed word selection, morphological elaboration, different spacing schedules, and digital flashcards.

Vocabulary selection

The selected words in this project are adapted from the HKUST STEM Vocabulary resource and cross-referenced with academic vocabulary records from the Corpus of Contemporary American English (COCA).

  • The HKUST resource supplied discipline-specific STEM vocabulary.
  • Project-recorded COCA fields supported academic-domain cross-referencing and part-of-speech information.
  • The resulting 138-word candidate pool was evaluated for instructional usefulness and perceived difficulty.
  • A 34-word subset was implemented in the digital-flashcard study.

Study design

  • The 34 target words are classified as simple, derived, or compound.
  • Recorded prefixes and suffixes support word-structure analysis.
  • The 2 × 2 between-participants design crosses spacing schedule (distributed or massed) with morphological elaboration (with or without morphology).
  • Participants encounter each target word three times.

Expert ratings

Expert raters evaluated instructional usefulness and perceived difficulty on 7-point scales. The website reports word-level means where these data are available.

Research status

The learning study is ongoing. Preliminary analyses are not presented here as finalized findings.
